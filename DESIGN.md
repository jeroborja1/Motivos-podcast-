## Brand & Style

This design system elevates biographical audio storytelling and authentic dialogues into an intimate, editorial-grade visual sanctuary. Designed around the ethos of *Historias reales. Motivos reales.*, the visual treatment eschews impersonal, tech-heavy podcast aesthetics in favor of a warm, human, literary presence grounded in craftsmanship, emotional depth, and moral authority.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Intimate Gravitas:** The quiet assurance of late-night spoken word, recorded in quiet rooms with close microphones.
- **Editorial Authority:** Cultivated pacing, literary hierarchy, and archival restraint reminiscent of prestige essays and cinematic documentaries.
- **Vulnerable & Grounded:** Earth-born hues and unhurried negative space that welcome listeners into personal revelations without melodrama.

### Target Audience
Reflective listeners, lifelong learners, founders, and culture creators seeking substantive personal narratives, candid reflections on life transitions, and grounded guidance over surface-level hustle motivation.

### Design Aesthetic
An editorial-humanist style combining:
- Warm paper-like surfaces (broken white, raw parchment, warm ivory) for enduring reading and listening comfort.
- Meticulous classical typography paired with functional, contemporary geometry.
- Tactile, quiet borders and atmospheric warm-tinted shadows that evoke linen-bound journals and bespoke audio records.

## Colors

The color architecture is derived from physical materials: sun-cured terracotta clay, aged amber lacquer, charcoal ink, and raw linen canvas.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#C26D45` - Terracotta Artisan):** Acts as the resonant focal point. Used for core interactive states, progress rings, current episode indicators, and key calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#D4973B` - Warm Golden Amber):** Evokes acoustic warmth, studio lamplight, and editorial prestige. Reserved for accolades, badges, timestamps, audio wave highlights, and quote callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#2D6A4F` - Deep Sage Laurel):** A calm, reassuring botanical emerald used specifically for direct human connection, community conversion touchpoints (e.g., WhatsApp VIP access, community notes), and resolved state tokens.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#1E1A18` - Deep Carbon):** A warm, ink-heavy black replacing pure digital black (`#000000`) across all body copy and primary headlines to preserve optical comfort and prevent eye fatigue.
- **Background & Canvas (`#FAF6F0` - Broken Warm Ivory):** The resting canvas mimicking heavyweight uncoated archival paper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Cream):** Used for elevated cards, listening bars, and drawer containers to delineate depth gently against the ivory base.
- **Subtle Stroke (`#E8DFC8` / `#E6DDD2`):** Soft, low-contrast borders reinforcing tactile containers without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites the introspective, literary grace of **Newsreader** with the engineered precision and effortless legibility of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Titles & Story Quotes (Newsreader):** Used for host statements, guest names, episode titles, and memorable story excerpts. Its fluid italic variants (`font-style: italic`) should be embraced selectively for epigraphs, guest attributions, and emotional inflection.
- **Narrative & Functional Copy (Manrope):** Applied to episode synopses, track times, show notes, and UI controls. Its geometric clarity balances the traditional serif presence, providing an optimal reading cadence on retina displays and mobile viewports.
- **Tracking Discipline:** Larger Newsreader headlines maintain negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create a cohesive editorial lockup. Category indicators and episode indices utilize `label-caps` with uppercase tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce structural order.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric editorial grid built upon an 8px base rhythm, pairing generous vertical negative space with compact metadata clusters.

### Structural Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum container width of `1240px` and `32px` gutters. Story-driven essay sections and show transcripts switch to an off-center single column constrained to `680px` for optimal typographic reading cadence.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `24px` gutters and margins. Sidebars collapse under primary episode content.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `20px` lateral margins. Audio players anchor permanently to the screen bottom with full-width horizontal bleed and tap targets exceeding `48px`.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm is deliberately unhurried. Hero sections utilize `space-4xl` (`6rem`) separation to allow portraiture and narrative themes to breathe, while conversational episode rows maintain tight, cohesive internal padding (`space-lg`) to foster intimacy.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids cold, artificial drop shadows, relying instead on warm light refraction and layered surfaces that feel physical and grounded.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **The Canvas (Base):** `#FAF6F0` (Ivory Linen). Pure flat surface without shadow.
2. **Resting Containers (Tier 1):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft amber-carbon perimeter shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 26, 24, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(194, 109, 69, 0.03);`
   Coupled with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(30, 26, 24, 0.07)`.
3. **Interactive Hover & Persistent Audio Dock (Tier 2):** Elevated interactive components rise slightly (`translateY(-2px)`) with expanded warm diffusion:
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 26, 24, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(194, 109, 69, 0.06);`
4. **Modal Overlays & Drawer Menus (Tier 3):** High-level panels float above a backdrop blur:
   `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.88);`
   `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(30, 26, 24, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The interface balances organic human warmth with architectural balance using a moderate level-2 roundedness profile.

- **Cards & Listening Panels:** Styled with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) corners, creating polished containers that soften photography without appearing childish or bubbly.
- **Interactive Buttons & Chips:** Standard interactive elements employ `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) corners for tactile authority, while live audio badges and playback pill-triggers use fully rounded profiles (`9999px`) to visually signify streaming movement.
- **Editorial Badges & Guest Portraits:** Guest portrait frames adopt `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) or subtle archival arched silhouettes to reinforce the narrative gravitas of real people.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Listen / Subscribe):** Background `#C26D45`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-interactive`, border radius `0.5rem`. Subtle hover brings a warm amber glow (`box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(194, 109, 69, 0.35)`) and smooth translation (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (Show Notes / Episodes):** Transparent base, border `1.5px solid #1E1A18`, text `#1E1A18`. Hover state fills with warm linen tint `rgba(194, 109, 69, 0.08)` and stroke transitions to `#C26D45`.
- **Conversion Trigger (WhatsApp / Direct Circle):** Background `#2D6A4F`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon integration. Reserved exclusively for direct host connection and VIP listener community channels.

### Cards & Episode Modules
- **Featured Episode Card:** Split-panel layout. Left side anchors high-contrast monochrome or warm duotone guest photography; right side houses `label-caps` category indicator in `#D4973B`, `headline-md` title in Newsreader, and interactive playback scrubber.
- **Archival Grid Card:** White surface, hairline border `rgba(30, 26, 24, 0.07)`, padded at `1.5rem`. Features quick-play circular trigger (`#C26D45`), duration chip, and guest quote in Newsreader italic.

### Custom Audio Player Dock
- Persistent docked bar at the viewport base.
- Features real-time waveform progress visualized through alternating vertical bars shifting from muted neutral (`#E8DFC8`) to rich terracotta (`#C26D45`).
- Includes speed toggles (1x, 1.2x, 1.5x) in refined `Manrope` monospace and direct chapter skipping markers.

### Input Fields & Subscriptions
- Cream background (`#FFFFFF`), inset padding `1rem 1.25rem`, border `1px solid rgba(30, 26, 24, 0.15)`.
- Focused state eliminates generic blue halos, adopting a crisp 1.5px border of `#C26D45` with an ambient glow of `rgba(194, 109, 69, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Tags
- Metadata tags (e.g., *Resiliencia*, *Vocación*, *Episodio 42*): Background `rgba(212, 151, 59, 0.12)`, text `#9C6A18`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 151, 59, 0.25)`, border radius `9999px`.
